The gate that mattered

The north-star gate returned COMPLETE on all seven criteria and then reported the north-star itself was not true. The backlog had grown to twenty rows — four added by the same day’s other retro, plus one half of an older item — and five were dispositioned advice while stating only where they landed, never why no check reaches them. Every criterion passed while the sentence they exist to make true was false for a quarter of the file.

Recording that as a caveat was available and defensible: the goal was chartered on fourteen items and the extra rows arrived from elsewhere. It would also have been reading a criterion generously at its own gate — the failure this goal landed a rule against in P3, in the document the gate was reading at the time. The gap was closed instead, and a second pass confirmed the claim now holds for every row.
