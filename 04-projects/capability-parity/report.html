Capability parity

ultragoal capability-parity · lane full · 2026-09-01 · outcome shipped

North star

Every capability the roster’s governance documents describe now exists, or the documents say it does not. Before this goal, the governance surface promised a retention regime, a deletion-with-evidence lifecycle and seven CLI verbs that a rewrite had removed weeks earlier — and the CLI answered unknown subcommand to all of them. The binary now answers every documented verb by name with what replaced it, a test fails if a document ever names a verb that does not exist, and the two remaining absences are declared in every document that touches them, with what they cost and what would restore them.

Four rounds

P2 and P3 were built once and verified four times. Each failure had a different cause, and the sequence is the most useful thing this goal produced.

RoundVerdictCause
1FAILFixed the locations the report named; never enumerated the set.
2FAILThe same, and introduced a verbatim duplicate paragraph through a slice-order edit — the stale copy standing 19 lines above its own correction.
3FAILEnumerated, but by removed-verb name. Four documents assert the capabilities without naming any command — “TTL-based expiration”, “does have deletion capability” — so no name-based search could reach them.
4PASSEnumerated by capability concept, plus a mechanical near-duplicate detector.

Round 3’s fix commit made the name-versus-concept mistake in the same message that diagnosed it as round 2’s root cause. Naming a failure mode did not protect against it. The two things that worked were both mechanical: an enumeration built before any editing, and a similarity check that does not depend on choosing the right search term — it found all four duplicate-paragraph instances at once, after hand-checking had missed each of them in turn.

The worst single defect sat outside every phase’s assumed scope. RELEASE_NOTES_PHASE4.md was at the repository root announcing retention and deletion as COMPLETE and Production Ready, last touched roughly two hours before the commit that removed those exact commands, updated never, linked from nowhere. AC-3 and AC-4 say every document, and that word is what eventually reached it.
